# Plan: Lemonade
*Drafted by the first doer, by PR; every change goes by PR. Nothing here is decided until Priya says yes (DECISIONS.md). The README stage table reads its stages from here.*

## Goal and done-when
- Goal: find out whether pre-orders are worth offering at the stand, and give the flavours page a phone-first redesign.
- Must-haves (done when): the committee has said yes or no to pre-orders, and the new flavours page is live and has been looked at on a phone.

## Stages
*Each stage ends in a milestone: a review with Priya, with a plain recap plus the result itself.*
| # | Stage | Done when | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pre-order research | Q1 to Q3 in research/START-HERE.md each have a dossier with a READY verdict | Priya reads the three dossiers' plain-words sections with Cyrus |
| 2 | New flavours page | A committee member has merged the PR and Cyrus has checked the live page | Priya opens the live page on her phone |
| 3 | Committee decision on pre-orders | The committee has answered the proposal | Priya and Cyrus go through the answer and decide whether to build anything |

## Order of work
Stages 1 and 2 run side by side: different chats, different files. Stage 3 starts when the dossiers have landed and Priya has decided to send a proposal. Nothing for pre-orders is built before the committee says yes (D3).

## What we need from others
- The committee: someone to review the flavours page PR, and a committee member to merge it (not on a Saturday morning, while the stand is open).
- Priya: the committee's rules for changing how the stand takes orders (for Q3).

## Top risks
| Risk | What it would do | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| A merge publishes at once | A broken flavours page is public until someone reverts it | RUNBOOK.md: preview first, full-tier review, no merge on a Saturday morning |
| The demand count measures questions, not paying customers | A wrong yes on pre-orders | The dossier marks demand "partly"; the proposal is a small trial, not a build |

## What could go wrong with the plan itself
The committee may not meet before the end of the month, so stage 3 could slip. Stage 2 could be held up by review, which sits with people outside the team.
