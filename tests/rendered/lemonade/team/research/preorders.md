## In plain words
Customers do ask to order ahead, about three a Saturday, and on five of the last eight Saturdays the two favourite flavours ran out before 2 pm. That points to real interest. But the notebook counts questions, not people who would pay and come back, so demand is only partly shown. Our view, **[unbacked]**: a small trial would teach more than more reading.

## 1. What the stand's order notebook shows
