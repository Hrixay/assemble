## 2026-10-15 · A four-week "reserve a cup" trial · Status: drafted
- Idea: for four Saturdays, customers can reserve a cup of the two favourite flavours on the site, with no payment, and collect it from 10 am. After four weeks the committee looks at how many reservations were collected and decides whether to keep it.
- Why: the two favourites sold out before 2 pm on 5 of the last 8 Saturdays, and 23 customers asked about ordering ahead in those 8 weeks (preorders.md, F1 and F2, confirmed). Whether those customers would have paid or come back is not found (F3), so a trial is the cheapest way to learn.
- Risk: reservations nobody collects; a volunteer has to read the reservation list on a busy morning; it changes the site from a static page to one that takes a form.
- Open questions for the owner: Who would read the list on Saturday morning? Does the committee allow a form on the site? Can the stand make more of the two favourites on a trial Saturday?
- Draft: "Hello all. Over the last 8 Saturdays 23 customers asked if they could order ahead, and our two favourite flavours sold out before 2 pm on 5 of them. We would like to suggest a four-week trial: a reserve-a-cup form on the flavours page, no payment, collect from 10 am. We would only build it if you agree, and we would stop after four weeks if it is not helping. What do you think? Priya"
  Draft for discussion only, not a request to build.

## 2026-10-12 · A "sold out" switch on the flavours page · Status: parked
- Idea: volunteers mark a flavour as sold out from a phone, and the flavours page shows it.
- Why: customers walk over to find a flavour gone (from Priya, 2026-10-12).
- Risk: someone has to remember to flip it; the page would need somewhere to keep that state, so it stops being a static page.
- Open questions for the owner: Would the committee want a login for volunteers? Who would look after it?
